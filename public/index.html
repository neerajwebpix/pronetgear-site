How buying works

No checkout and no guesswork. You talk to the team that used the equipment.

Pick your items

Browse by category or search by model. Every page lists the SKU, quantity, condition and any notes we hold.

Message us the SKU

Use the WhatsApp, call or email buttons on any listing. We reply with price and what is in the box.

Confirm the details

We confirm quantity, test status and accessories. Ask us when you enquire; all equipment can be inspected and tested on site before purchase.

Why buy ex-event equipment

This stock was deployed at corporate events, then retired into our store. We list what we know and flag what we do not: tested units are marked, untested units are marked, and for-parts units are clearly labelled. Exact age is not recorded for most items.

Looking for something in bulk? Our bulk lots page covers assorted lots we have not itemised yet.

Not sure what you need?

Describe your network or event and we will point you to compatible listings.
