About this item

This listing covers 30 units of the SC 1.25G SFP Module, 1310 nm (LB, Dual). The stock was used at corporate events and is now surplus from our own event inventory, so you are buying from the team that deployed it.

SFP modules are small optical or copper transceivers that plug into switch and router ports to carry fibre links.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put an SFP module to work:

  • Fibre uplinks between switches
  • Linking buildings or floors over fibre
  • Replacing a failed or missing module
  • Event back-haul over leased fibre

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Matching wavelength and connector at both ends of the link
  • Fibre type: single-mode or multi-mode
  • Compatibility with your switch vendor
