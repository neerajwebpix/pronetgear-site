Point-to-point wireless radios link two locations without trenching cable.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put a wireless radio to work:

  • Linking a venue to a remote building or tower
  • Backup or temporary links between sites
  • Extending a network across a campus or open ground
  • Short-term event back-haul

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Frequency band and legal use in your location
  • Line of sight between the two ends
  • Whether both units of the pair are included and powered with the correct injectors
