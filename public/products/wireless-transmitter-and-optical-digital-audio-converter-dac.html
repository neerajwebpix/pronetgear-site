About this item

This listing covers 1 unit of the Wireless Transmitter and Optical Digital Audio Converter (DAC). Condition is not recorded for this listing yet. We confirm it on enquiry.

A wireless transmitter with a digital-to-analogue converter (DAC) takes digital optical audio, for example from a TV or set-top box, and sends it to Bluetooth devices or turns it into analogue sound for amplifiers and speakers.

Good to know: Brand and model are not marked on the unit. Specifications are read from the markings on the unit. Optical, 3.5 mm and RCA cables are shown in the photos.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put a digital audio converter to work:

  • Sending TV audio to Bluetooth headphones or speakers
  • Feeding an older amplifier or speakers that have only RCA or AUX inputs
  • Passing optical audio through to another device
  • Adding wireless audio to a home theatre or event set-up

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Which input you need: optical or AUX
  • Whether your receiving device supports the Bluetooth link you plan to use
  • Power adapter and cables supplied
