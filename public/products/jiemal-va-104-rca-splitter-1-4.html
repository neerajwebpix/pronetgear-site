RCA (composite) distribution amplifiers copy one analogue video or audio source to several outputs.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put an RCA splitter to work:

  • Feeding analogue video to several monitors
  • Older cameras and DVD or tape sources
  • Duplicating audio feeds in small setups
  • Legacy AV rooms

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Number of outputs
  • Power adapter included
  • Whether it handles video, audio or both
