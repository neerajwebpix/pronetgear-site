About this item

This listing covers 15 units of the Hikvision AcuSense CCTV Bullet Camera. Condition and purchase details are not recorded for this listing yet. We confirm them on enquiry.

Bullet cameras are long, weather-resistant network cameras that mount on walls and eaves and point at a fixed view. AcuSense is Hikvision's analytics feature that tells people and vehicles apart from other motion, which cuts false alerts.

Good to know: One of the photographed boxes is an Ultra Series 8 MP bullet camera, and its label reads DS-2CD3086G2-IS. We will confirm the exact model for all 15 units on enquiry.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put a CCTV bullet camera to work:

  • Perimeter, gate and parking-area monitoring
  • Shop-front, warehouse and loading-bay coverage
  • Reducing false alarms on sites where leaves, insects or lights trigger plain motion detection
  • Adding or replacing cameras on an existing Hikvision NVR system

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Exact model and resolution of the units you receive (confirm from the label)
  • PoE or 12 V power: confirm your switch, injector or recorder supplies what the camera needs
  • Compatibility with your NVR and its firmware
  • Mounting bracket and cable pigtail included
