About this item

This listing covers 5 units of the Hikvision EXIR Fixed Turret Network Camera. Condition and purchase details are not recorded for this listing yet. We confirm them on enquiry.

Turret (eyeball) cameras are compact fixed cameras that mount on a ceiling or wall. EXIR is Hikvision's infrared night-vision lighting, and H.265+ compresses video so recordings use less storage.

Good to know: Model, resolution and lens are read from the label of the photographed box. We confirm they match all 5 units on enquiry.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put a CCTV turret camera to work:

  • Offices, reception areas and corridors
  • Shops, showrooms and small warehouses
  • Entrances and covered outdoor areas
  • Adding cameras to an existing Hikvision recording system

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Exact model and resolution (confirm from the box or unit label)
  • PoE or 12 V power supply
  • Compatibility with your NVR and firmware
  • Mounting base and screws included
