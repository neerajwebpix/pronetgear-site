Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put a bluetooth audio adapter to work:

  • Listening to a TV on Bluetooth headphones
  • Streaming from a phone to a wired speaker or home stereo
  • Adding Bluetooth to a mixer, amplifier or car AUX input
  • Sharing one source with two headphones

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:
