About this item

This listing covers 1 unit of the Milestone ML-102 HDMI Splitter. The stock was used at corporate events and is now surplus from our own event inventory, so you are buying from the team that deployed it.

An HDMI splitter sends one source to several displays at once.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put an HDMI splitter to work:

  • Mirroring a presenter laptop to multiple screens
  • Exhibition and showroom displays
  • Feeding a recorder and a monitor from one source
  • Training rooms and conferences

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Number of outputs and maximum resolution
  • Power adapter included
  • HDCP behaviour with your source
