About this item

This listing covers 66 units of the PoE Injector / Adapter (802.3af/at). The stock was used at corporate events and is now surplus from our own event inventory, so you are buying from the team that deployed it.

A PoE injector sends power and data down one Ethernet cable so an access point or radio does not need a nearby power socket.

Good to know: This is a mixed lot of 66 units. Each brand has its own price, listed in the specifications table. The photos show examples from the lot. Tell us the brand and quantity you need and we will confirm what is in stock.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put a PoE adapter to work:

  • Powering access points where the switch has no PoE
  • Powering outdoor radios and cameras
  • Event deployments with long cable runs
  • Spare injectors for field kits

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Output voltage and PoE standard against the device you want to power
  • Whether the power cord or adapter is included
  • Maximum cable length for your use
