About this item

This listing covers 7 units of the Ubiquiti UAP-AC Outdoor Access Point. The stock was used at corporate events and is now surplus from our own event inventory, so you are buying from the team that deployed it.

Outdoor-class access points are built to live on poles, walls and truss, which is why they turned up so often on event deployments.

Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put an outdoor access point to work:

  • High-density event and exhibition Wi-Fi where coverage is split across many small cells
  • Campus, stadium-seating and open-ground coverage
  • Warehouse, factory-yard and parking-lot Wi-Fi
  • Temporary or rental networks that get redeployed repeatedly

Before you buy: what to check

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Which controller or management software the unit expects, and whether any licence is needed
  • PoE source: confirm your switch or injector supplies the power this model needs
  • Mounting hardware: confirm brackets are included
