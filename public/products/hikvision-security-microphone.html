Where it is useful

Typical ways buyers put a security microphone to work:

  • Reception desks, counters and meeting areas where audio helps the record
  • Shops and offices that already use a Hikvision recorder
  • Warehouse and gate-house monitoring
  • Adding sound to an existing camera system

Used equipment is easy to get right if you check a few things first:

  • Whether your camera or recorder has an audio input and what it expects
  • Power requirement and cable length
  • Exact model (confirm from the unit label)
